Welcome to my AI engineering portfolio. My work focuses on building transparent, high-performance AI systems from first
principles. I believe that truly robust AI orchestration requires a deep understanding of the underlying mechanics —
from the calculus of backpropagation to the distributed orchestration of large-scale models.

This section documents a series of technical projects where I've built the core components of modern AI from scratch,
focusing on architectural clarity, mathematical precision, and engineering at scale.

---

### Core Engineering Projects

- **[AI Core Concepts & Technical Deep Dive](/ai/core-concepts/)**  
  A comprehensive multi-chapter narrative covering the mathematical foundations, architectural evolution, and
  high-performance engineering of modern AI.

- **[Language Modelling](/ai/language-modelling/)**
  A journey through the architectural milestones of NLP. Tracing the path from frequentist Bigram models and Multi-Layer
  Perceptrons to hierarchical WaveNet architectures that capture deep temporal dependencies.

- **[Scalar Autograd Engine](/ai/autograd-engine/)**  
  A custom-built automatic differentiation engine that implements backpropagation for scalar values. This project
  demystifies the "black box" of neural network training by building the foundational DAG-based gradient engine from the
  ground up.

- **[Transformer Architecture & GPT-2](/ai/transformer-architecture/)**  
  A from-scratch implementation of the GPT-2 decoder-only transformer. This project explores causal self-attention,
  multi-head attention mechanisms, and the structural innovations that allow transformers to model complex linguistic
  patterns.

- **[Distributed Training Infrastructure](/ai/distributed-training-infrastructure/)**  
  Scaling AI training to professional levels. This project implements Distributed Data Parallel (DDP), gradient
  accumulation, and mixed-precision (BF16/TF32) optimizations to orchestrate model training across high-performance GPU
  clusters.

- **[Professional PyTorch Workflow](/ai/pytorch-engineering-workflow/)**  
  A robust, reproducible framework for AI development. Focuses on device-agnostic programming, deterministic research,
  and the rigorous engineering patterns required to move from experimental scripts to production-ready models.

- **[Reinforcement Learning & Autonomous Agents](/ai/reinforcement-learning/)**  
  Developing goal-oriented systems that navigate stochastic environments. This project explores Deep Q-Networks (DQN)
  and Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) to build agents that respect physical laws and conservation constraints.
